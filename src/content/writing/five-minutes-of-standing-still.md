I don't chase perfect light. I wait for the moment it turns honest.

Most of the frames in the [photography](/photography/) section weren't planned. I walked until something insisted on being photographed, then stood still for five minutes while the light, the gesture, or the silhouette fell into place.

The same instinct turns out to be useful in engineering: slow down long enough to see what the system is actually doing before you change it.
